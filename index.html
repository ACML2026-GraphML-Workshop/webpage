About The Workshop

Learning from graphs, networks, and higher-order relational structures.

Graph machine learning has become a fundamental paradigm for learning from structured and relational data across artificial intelligence, data mining, science, and engineering. Recent advances have expanded the capability of graph neural networks and related models, while raising critical questions around theory, scalability, robustness, distribution shift, trustworthiness, and foundation models for graphs.

GML 2026 brings together researchers at different career stages to present new ideas, discuss open problems, and foster collaborations toward the next generation of graph machine learning.

Call For Papers

Submissions will be handled through a dedicated OpenReview venue.

GML 2026 invites submissions on foundations, models, systems, and applications of graph machine learning. Each submission will receive at least two reviews and will be evaluated for relevance, originality, technical quality, clarity, and potential impact. Accepted contributions may be presented as oral presentations, spotlight talks, or posters, subject to the final ACML workshop programme.
